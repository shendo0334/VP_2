# VP Gold & Diamonds design system

## Composition

The showroom identity is expressed through dark plum, gold metal, and parchment. Use the supplied VP logo at its original proportions on a dark plum background. Frame the hero with a narrow ivory gutter and place copy in quiet space across the left. Use wide editorial photography with warm directional light and close jewellery detail. The reference recording guides the shifts between full photography, light breathing space, and a dark service panel.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#f8f4ee` | Page surface |
| Ivory | `#fffaf3` | Elevated light surfaces and reverse text |
| Ink | `#25191e` | Headings and body |
| Plum | `#321623` | Footer and feature panels |
| Burgundy | `#512738` | Primary dark actions and dark-toned backgrounds |
| Gold | `#e8c888` | Filled actions on dark, lines and highlights |
| Bronze | `#745123` | Labels and accent text on light |
| Muted | `#695a60` | Supporting copy |
| Hairline | `#dfd3c7` | Cards, rules and dividers |

Use Instrument Serif for headlines, DM Sans for navigation and body, and Noto Sans Malayalam for passages marked `lang="ml"`. Display sizes scale from 60 to 112 px on the home hero, 52 to 88 px on inner page leads and 42 to 66 px for sections. Body defaults to 16 px with 1.6 leading. Use 13 px uppercase tracking for editorial labels and 14 px or larger for functional controls. Set a comfortable Malayalam line-height near 1.8.

Spacing follows 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 88 and 112 px. The outer shell tops out at 1512 px and the inner content grid at 1240 px. Use responsive gutters from 16 to 48 px. Grid columns change from one on narrow routes to two at 640 px and three or four when the content fits at 1024 px. The full desktop navigation starts at 1160 px. Prefer hairline borders, small media radii, and light shadows only where depth clarifies the interface.

## Controls and motion

Use capsule CTAs with 44 px minimum height. Fill the primary action in burgundy or gold depending on the surface. Secondary actions use a visible outline or underlined text. The header keeps Shop Now visible at all sizes. A compact navigation disclosure supports keyboard activation and Escape.

Use 840 ms staggered hero entrances with 100 ms offsets. Section content enters once with an 18 px rise over about 680 ms using cubic ease-out. Inline interactions run around 200 ms. Media hover uses a slow 1.045 zoom. Reduced-motion preferences disable travel, stagger, and smooth scroll. Keep focus rings prominent and content visible with JavaScript disabled.
